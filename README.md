# Receipt POS

Transform any Android phone or tablet into a high-speed, enterprise-grade Point of Sale (POS) register. Built for retail shops, supermarkets, cafes, restaurants, boutiques, pharmacies, and multi-counter businesses, Receipt POS combines lightning-fast checkout, native ESC/POS thermal printing, external barcode gun support, smart credit notes, and multi-device Google Drive sync in one zero-latency offline-first application.

## Why business owners choose Receipt POS

- 100% Offline-First Reliability: Never lose a sale when the internet drops. Process bills, print receipts, and track stock completely offline.
- Multi-Counter Collision-Free Billing: Run multiple billing counters (C1, C2, C3, C4+) simultaneously under a single Google Drive account without invoice or credit note numbering conflicts.
- Hardware-Ready Out of the Box: Plug-and-play support for 58mm & 80mm Bluetooth and Wi-Fi/LAN thermal printers, cash drawers, and USB OTG / Bluetooth barcode scanners.

## Key functional capabilities

### 1. Lightning-fast register & smart checkout
- Instant Product Lookup: Search by product name, SKU, category, or barcode in milliseconds.
- Flexible Discounts & Taxes: Apply item-level percentage discounts, whole-cart promotional discounts, and configurable regional tax rates (GST, VAT, Sales Tax) with toggleable tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive billing.
- Custom Quick-Add Items: Instantly bill custom or uncatalogued items on the fly during peak hours.
- Hold & Recall Bills: Park incomplete customer carts and resume them anytime without slowing down the checkout queue.

### 2. Complete payment collection & auditable transaction IDs
- Multi-Mode Payment Support: Accept Cash, UPI / Dynamic QR Code, Credit Card, Debit Card, and Contactless / NFC payments.
- Dynamic UPI QR Generation: Automatically generates a scannable payment QR code pre-filled with your merchant UPI ID and exact bill total.
- Mandatory Digital Transaction Tracking: Enforces Transaction ID / Reference capture for UPI, Credit Card, and Debit Card payments (and optional capture for Contactless/NFC)—printed directly on thermal receipts and PDF invoices to prevent payment fraud and simplify end-of-day reconciliation.
- Smart Cash Tender Calculator: Quick-denomination buttons and instant change-due calculation.

### 3. Native 58mm & 80mm ESC/POS thermal printing
- Dual Connectivity: Connect effortlessly to Bluetooth Classic/BLE thermal printers or high-speed Wi-Fi / Ethernet LAN printers (Port 9100).
- Custom Receipt Branding: Personalize headers, taglines, store address, tax registration IDs (GSTIN/VAT), footers, and denomination currency logos.
- Cryptographic Receipt Verification: Prints tamper-evident security hashes, scannable verification QR codes, and Code-128 invoice barcodes on every slip.
- Hardware Diagnostics & Peripherals: Built-in RJ11 Cash Drawer Kick pulse, automatic Paper Cut command, printer self-test page, and live ESC/POS byte spooler logs.
- Digital PDF Invoices & Instant Sharing: Generate crisp A4/thermal PDF invoices and share via WhatsApp, Email, or system print with one tap.

### 4. Seamless barcode scanning (camera + external hardware guns)
- External Barcode Scanner Hub: Dedicated Scanner tab supporting USB OTG HID keyboard-wedge scanners, Bluetooth HID barcode guns, and Bluetooth SPP serial scanners.
- Auto-Add to Cart: Automatically intercepts rapid hardware barcode bursts from anywhere on the register screen, matches the SKU/barcode against your catalog, and increments the bill quantity hands-free.
- Built-In Camera Scanner: High-speed ML Kit camera barcode & QR scanner for mobile checkout and inventory lookup.

### 5. Returns, refunds & smart credit notes (trade notes)
- Itemized Partial or Full Returns: Select specific items and quantities from any historical invoice to process accurate returns and automatic stock restoration.
- Printable Credit Notes: Issue official Credit Notes (e.g., CN-C1-261027-1001) with scannable barcodes and track remaining balances.
- One-Scan Credit Redemption: Redeem active customer Credit Notes directly in the Collect Payment dialog by scanning the Credit Note barcode or selecting from active customer credits.
- PIN-Protected Refund Security: Optional Master PIN lock to restrict unauthorized refunds and voids.

### 6. Multi-counter & multi-device Google Drive sync
- Terminal Counter IDs: Assign unique Counter IDs (C1, C2, C3, C4, or custom) to each register. Every Invoice (INV-C1-YYMMDD-1001) and Credit Note (CN-C1-YYMMDD-1001) carries its counter prefix so multiple devices never overwrite each other's bills.
- Automated Cloud Backup & Restore: Sync products, customers, invoices, credit notes, and store configurations to a dedicated Google Drive folder with collision-free multi-device merging.

### 7. Inventory, barcode studio & Excel/CSV bulk tools
- Real-Time Stock Tracking: Automatic stock deduction on sale, low-stock warning badges, and out-of-stock protection.
- Built-In Barcode Label Studio: Generate and print custom product barcode stickers with SKU, product name, and price.
- One-Tap CSV / Excel Import & Export: Bulk-import entire product catalogs or export inventory and sales ledgers for accounting.

### 8. Multi-business profiles, CRM & executive analytics
- Multi-Store Switching: Manage multiple businesses or branches from a single device with isolated catalogs, tax rules, and branding.
- Customer Directory (CRM): Track customer phone numbers, purchase history, loyalty points, and lifetime spend.
- Live Sales Analytics Dashboard: Monitor daily/monthly revenue, net profit margins, average order value, top-selling products, and payment method breakdowns at a glance.
