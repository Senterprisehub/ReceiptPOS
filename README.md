# ReceiptPOS
Smart Thermal POS & Billing Suite
The All-in-One Offline-First Point of Sale, Inventory, Credit Note & Cloud Backup Solution for Modern Retail & Hospitality
Smart Thermal POS turns any Android phone or tablet into a fast, secure, and enterprise-grade billing counter. Designed for retail stores, supermarkets, cafes, restaurants, boutiques, electronics shops, and multi-outlet businesses, it works 100% offline with zero lag while keeping your entire business backed up to your own Google Drive.
Why Store Owners Choose Smart Thermal POS
#1. Lightning-Fast Billing & Checkout (Register)
Adaptive Touch Register: Works seamlessly on both compact smartphones and wide-screen tablets (split-screen catalog and cart view).
Smart Search & Category Filtering: Find items instantly by product name, SKU, or barcode.
Flexible Discounts & Tips: Apply item-level discounts (Percentage % or Flat amount) as well as full-bill discounts and optional tips.
Multi-Mode Payments: Accept Cash (with instant change calculation and quick denomination buttons), Card, Digital Wallet, or Dynamic UPI QR Code.
Dynamic UPI QR Code Generator: Automatically generates a scannable payment QR code on screen with the exact bill total and your Payee Display Name & UPI ID—eliminating manual typing errors by customers.
#2. Hassle-Free Partial Item Returns & Trade / Credit Notes (CN-...)
No More Full-Bill Cancellations for Single-Item Returns: If a customer wants to return just one or two items from a larger invoice, you never have to void the entire bill.
Phone-Number Based Bill Lookup: Simply enter the customer’s registered mobile number in the Receipts section to pull up their past invoices, select the exact item(s) and quantity being returned, and issue an official Credit / Trade Note (CN-...).
Automatic Stock Restoration: Returned item quantities are automatically added back to your live inventory stock.
Print Now or Adjust Later: Print or share a standalone Credit Note Invoice (Thermal Receipt or A4 PDF) if the customer isn't buying immediately.
Automatic Bill Adjustment & Balance Carry-Forward: Next time the customer shops, entering their mobile number at checkout automatically detects their active Credit Note! It deducts the credit from the new bill, prints the exact Credit Note # and Original Bill # (Adjusted from Bill #INV-...) on the new invoice, and saves any leftover credit balance for future purchases.
#3. Universal Thermal Printing (Bluetooth & Wi-Fi) + A4 PDF Invoices
58mm & 80mm ESC/POS Thermal Support: Connect effortlessly to Bluetooth or Network/Wi-Fi thermal receipt printers with auto-reconnect and a built-in print spooler.
Custom Branding & Store Logo: Print your store logo, custom header/footer messages, tax ID/GSTIN, barcode, and cryptographic QR verification code on every receipt.
Professional A4 PDF & Excel Exports: Generate, print, or share clean A4 PDF tax invoices via WhatsApp/Email, or bulk-export your sales register to Excel (.csv/spreadsheet) and Batch PDFs for accounting in one tap.
#4. Multi-Business / Multi-Store Management
Run Multiple Stores from One App: Switch between multiple business profiles (e.g., a Retail Mart, a Coffee Shop, or a Pharmacy) from the top bar.
Isolated Catalogs & Tax Rules: Each business profile maintains its own product catalog, store logo, currency, tax percentage, and receipt numbering.
Global Currency & Tax Presets: Built-in presets for major global currencies (₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP, AED, etc.) and regional tax labels (GST, VAT, Sales Tax).
#5. Live Inventory & Catalog Control
Complete Product Management: Track selling price, cost price, SKU, barcode, tax rate, item color tags, and live stock quantities.
Real-Time Stock Deduction & Alerts: Stock updates automatically on every sale, refund, void, or partial item return.
#6. Built-In Customer Directory (CRM)
Customer Profiles by Mobile Number: Save customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, and notes.
Purchase & Credit Tracking: Track total customer spend, visit count, invoice history, and active Credit Note balances tied to their mobile number.
#7. Bank-Grade Security & Tamper Protection
4-Digit Supervisor PIN Lock: Protect sensitive operations—such as Full Refunds, Voiding Invoices, Partial Item Returns / Credit Notes, Store Settings, and Database Resets—behind a hashed Supervisor PIN with brute-force lockout protection.
Cryptographic Receipt Integrity Verification: Every generated invoice is signed with a tamper-evident security hash (HMAC-SHA256) so altered or fake receipts are immediately flagged.
Target Google Account Lock: Lock your designated backup Gmail address in Settings so staff cannot accidentally change or redirect your store backups.
#8. Dual Backup: Local Storage + Personal Google Drive Sync
100% Data Ownership: Your data stays on your device and syncs directly to your own Google Drive—no expensive monthly cloud subscriptions required.
Complete Snapshot Backup & Restore: Every product, receipt, customer, store configuration, and Credit Note balance is preserved in both Local Encrypted/JSON Backups and Connected Google Drive Backups, making device upgrades effortless.
