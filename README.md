Smart Thermal POS & Billing Suite

The all-in-one offline-first point of sale, inventory, credit note, and cloud backup solution for modern retail and hospitality businesses.

Smart Thermal POS turns any Android phone or tablet into a fast, secure, and enterprise-grade billing counter for stores, supermarkets, cafes, restaurants, boutiques, and electronics shops.

## Why store owners choose Smart Thermal POS

### 1. Lightning-fast billing & checkout
- Adaptive touch register optimized for both compact phones and wide tablets
- Split-screen catalog and cart experience for faster order entry
- Smart search for items by product name, SKU, or barcode
- Flexible discounting with percentage or flat-rate adjustments
- Optional tips and multi-mode payments including cash, card, digital wallet, and UPI
- Dynamic UPI QR code generation with exact bill totals and payee information

### 2. Hassle-free partial returns & trade credit notes
- Return only the items you need instead of voiding entire bills
- Lookup past receipts using customer mobile number
- Apply partial returns with automatic stock restoration
- Print or share individual credit note invoices instantly
- Automatically carry forward active credit balances to future purchases

### 3. Universal thermal printing + A4 invoice export
- Support for 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi
- Auto-reconnect and print queue handling for reliable operations
- Custom store branding with logo, headers, footers, GST details, and QR verification
- Generate clean A4 PDFs and Excel exports for accounting and compliance

### 4. Multi-business & multi-store management
- Switch between independent business profiles from a single app
- Separate catalogs, tax rules, receipt numbering, and branding per store
- Built-in currency presets and regional tax label support

### 5. Live inventory & catalog control
- Full product management for pricing, stock, SKUs, barcodes, and tags
- Real-time deduction of stock on every sale, refund, return, or credit note
- Better visibility into inventory movement and item health

### 6. Built-in customer directory (CRM)
- Save customer profiles via phone number, email, address, and notes
- Track spend, visit counts, invoice history, and active credit balances

### 7. Bank-grade security & tamper protection
- 4-digit supervisor PIN lock for sensitive actions
- Hashed protection for refunds, voids, returns, settings changes, and database resets
- Cryptographic receipt integrity verification using HMAC-SHA256
- Targeted Google account lock to protect backup settings and data recovery

### 8. Dual backup: local storage + Google Drive sync
- Full ownership of your store data
- Local encrypted JSON backups and personal Google Drive sync
- Restore product, receipt, customer, store, and credit note data with confidence
